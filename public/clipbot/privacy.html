Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

GTACLIPS6 runs entirely on its owner's computer.

  • TikTok data used: the connected account's display name and avatar, its follower, like and video counts, and the list and statistics of its videos. This data is used only to show the dashboard and to post clips.
  • Storage: this data and the TikTok access tokens are stored in a local database on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to a third-party server, sold or shared.
  • Deletion: deleting the tool's data folder removes all stored data. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok app, under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.

Contact: open an issue on github.com/Ayski/Ayski.
