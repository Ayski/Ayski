GTACLIPS6

GTACLIPS6 is a personal desktop tool. It runs on its owner's computer, edits short vertical gaming highlight clips and posts them to the owner's own TikTok account.

  • The owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok's official login screen.
  • Each clip shows a permanent credit to the original streamer, in the video and in the caption.
  • The owner chooses the privacy level of every post.

Contact: open an issue on github.com/Ayski/Ayski.
